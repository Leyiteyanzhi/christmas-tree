How to play

  1. Choose your tree and room

    Start with an empty tree or a starter layout. Each tree keeps its own decorations.

  2. Decorate from any side

    Pick an ornament, drag anywhere to orbit around the tree, and tap a glowing spot to place it. Pinch or scroll to zoom, or use Fullscreen for an immersive view.

  3. Admire and save your tree

    Use Auto orbit to admire every side. Tap the scene to pause, then use Photo to save that view. Use Works to copy or download an editable project.
