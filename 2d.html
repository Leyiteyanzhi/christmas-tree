How to play

No timer, no perfect answer. Take your time and make a tree that feels like yours.

  1. Pick a tree and scene

    Choose a tree shape and background. Start from an empty tree, or continue with a starter layout. Each tree keeps its own draft; empty and starter replace the current layout and can be undone.

  2. Choose an ornament, then tap to hang it

    Pick from Ornaments, then tap a branch to place it. You can hang the same item again. Tree toppers need a tap near the tip; floor gifts and figures need a tap near the base.

  3. Nudge until it looks right

    Tap Done placing, then click or drag ornaments already on the tree. Selected items can be removed or rotated; baubles can change color. On overlaps, press and hold to pick the one you want; on desktop you can also right-click.

  4. Zoom in and turn on the lights

    Use − / + at the bottom right of the canvas, or tap the percentage to return to full view. On desktop, scroll to zoom and drag empty space after zooming; on phones, pinch to zoom and pan. Try warm or colored lights.

  5. Save your tree

    Use Photo to save a full view or close-up PNG. Use Works to save a copy in this browser or download an editable project file.

Keyboard shortcuts

Undo: Ctrl / ⌘ + Z. Redo: Ctrl / ⌘ + Shift + Z. Esc leaves placing or selection. Delete removes the selected ornament.

With the canvas focused, arrow keys move the marker and Enter places or selects. With an ornament selected, Alt + arrows nudge it; hold Shift for larger steps.
