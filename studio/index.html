How to play

  1. Choose your tree and room

    Start with an empty tree or a starter layout. Each tree keeps its own decorations.

  2. Decorate from any side

    Pick an ornament, then tap a glowing spot. Use Orbit view to walk around the tree. Pinch or scroll to zoom.

  3. Adjust and take a photo

    In Edit ornaments, select a decoration to move or remove it. Choose your lights and use Photo to save this view.
